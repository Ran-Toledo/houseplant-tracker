Screen D: Assistant

Design reference for phase 3. The assistant reads your plants and the species catalog and answers in text; it never changes anything. Three states: the Assistant tab, a conversation about one plant, and the plant page's entry point.

D1 · Assistant tab: conversations

Assistant

Ask about your plants. Answers use your care history and the species guides.

The assistant gives suggestions only. To log care or change a plant, use the plant's page.

Newest first. Plant conversations show the plant; general ones cover the whole collection. With no conversations yet, the page shows the starter questions from D2 under "New chat". Hidden from the navigation when AI features are off and there are no conversations.

Why are Monty's lower leaves turning yellow?
AI

Most likely too much water. Monty was watered 4 times in the last 10 days, more often than the Monstera guide's 5 to 9 days, and it sits in a plastic pot without a drainage hole.

  • Let the top quarter of the soil dry before the next watering.
  • Repot into a pot with drainage when you can.
  • Old lower leaves also yellow naturally, one at a time.

You can log the repotting from Monty's page.
