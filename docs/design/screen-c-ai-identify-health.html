Screen C: AI identification and health checks

Design reference for phase 2. Four states: identifying a plant in the plant form, a health check result, the Health checks list on the plant page, and the photo viewer's new button.

Choosing a catalog match fills the species picker. A match outside the catalog picks "Not in the catalog" and fills the species name. If the photo shows no plant, the panel says so instead of listing matches.

C2 · Plant page: health check result

Health checks

Needs attention

Just now · Medium confidence · AI

Mostly healthy growth, but a few lower leaves are yellowing and the soil looks wet.

Overwatering Likely

Two lower leaves are yellow with soft stems, and the topsoil looks dark and wet.

  • Let the top half of the soil dry before watering again.
  • Check that the drainage hole isn't blocked.
Too little light Possible

New leaves are smaller and the stems lean towards the window.

  • Move it closer to the window, out of direct midday sun.
The photo is a little dark, so the result may be less reliable.

AI assessment, not a diagnosis. Check the plant yourself before acting on it.

While it runs, the card shows the same progress state as C1 ("Checking the plant's health…"). "Check health" uses the cover photo; with no photo it asks for one and adds it to the plant's photos. The condition only changes when you tap the button, which then shows "Condition updated".

C3 · Plant page: past checks and photo viewer

Health checks

Newest first. Tapping a row opens its full result card (C2) in place.

Photo viewer: "Check health" runs a check on this photo, closes the viewer and shows the result in the Health checks section.
