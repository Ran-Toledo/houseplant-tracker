Screen E: Settings → Notifications

Design reference for phase 4. A daily push notification on the iPhone lists the plants whose soil check is due. The section adapts to where the app is opened: three states.

Notifications

Off

Notifications need the secure address of the app, installed on the Home Screen:

  1. On the iPhone, open the Tailscale app and connect.
  2. In Safari, open https://laptop-….ts.net
  3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the app from the Home Screen and come back here.

In-app reminders (the bell on My plants) work everywhere without this.

Shown when the page isn't a secure context. On iPhone Safari over https but not installed, step 3 alone is shown. If the server has no push keys, the card says notifications aren't set up on this computer.

E2 · Installed app, not enabled yet

Notifications

Off

Get one notification a day listing the plants whose soil is due for a check. Nothing is sent on days when no plant needs care.

The iPhone asks for permission. Notifications travel through Apple's push service; the computer has to be on at notification time.

If permission was refused earlier, the button is replaced by a note explaining how to allow it in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
